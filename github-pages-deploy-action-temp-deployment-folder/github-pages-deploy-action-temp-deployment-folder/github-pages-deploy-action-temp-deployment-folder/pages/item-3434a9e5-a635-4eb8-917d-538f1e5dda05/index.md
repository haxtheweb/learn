How to Add Video to Your Page: Multiple Methods Explained
---------------------------------------------------------

Prerequisites
-------------

Before you start, ensure you have **editing access** to the page. Prepare the **video link** (such as a YouTube URL) or have your **local video file** ready for upload.

Add Video Using the Media Block
-------------------------------

*   Click **Edit** on your page to enter editing mode.
*   Open the **blocks** panel and search for **Video** under the Media category.
*   Select the **Video** block to add it to your page.
*   Update the **source URL** if you want to embed a different YouTube video.
*   Click **Save** to publish the changes.

Embed Video by Pasting a Link
-----------------------------

*   While in edit mode, paste your **YouTube link** on a new, individual line.
*   The system will **automatically detect** the link and embed the video for you.

Insert Video Using the Slash Command
------------------------------------

*   Type **/video** while editing to invoke the video insertion command.
*   Insert the video and **update the URL** to your desired video.
*   Add a **title** in the title field for accessibility and clarity.
*   Optionally, **change the poster image** to a custom thumbnail.

Customize Video Settings
------------------------

*   Modify **spacing** around your video for better layout.
*   In the **Advanced** section, set up **text tracks** (captions/subtitles), choose if the video should **start at the beginning**, and add **audio descriptive tracks** if needed.

Create a Video Playlist
-----------------------

*   Drag one video on top of another to **automatically create a playlist**.
*   This feature is unique to videos and helps organize multiple clips in sequence.

Upload Local Videos or Use Screen Recorder
------------------------------------------

*   Use the **Video block** to upload a video file from your computer.
*   Alternatively, use the **in-context screen recorder** to capture and add a video directly.
*   Note: Screen recordings can result in **very large files**. Consider compressing before uploading.

Verification
------------

After adding your video, **preview the page** to ensure the video displays and plays correctly. Test all customizations, such as titles, poster images, and playlists, to confirm they work as expected.
