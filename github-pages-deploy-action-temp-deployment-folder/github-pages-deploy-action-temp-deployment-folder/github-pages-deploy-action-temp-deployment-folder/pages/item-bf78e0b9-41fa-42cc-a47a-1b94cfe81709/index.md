How to Create an Online Course in HAXcms
----------------------------------------

Prerequisites
-------------

*   Ensure you have access to the HAXCMS dashboard.
*   Be ready with a course name for your new online course.

Having these prerequisites will make your course creation process smooth and efficient.

Access the Online Course Creation Tool
--------------------------------------

From the HAXCMS dashboard, locate and click the **Online Course** option. This will open the course creation interface where you can start building your new course.

Name and Create Your Course
---------------------------

Enter your desired course name (for example, **HAX100**) in the provided field. You can either press **Enter** on your keyboard or click the **Create Site** button to proceed. The system will begin creating your course, indicated by a progress bar and a hat icon.

Review and Manage Your New Course
---------------------------------

*   Once your course is created, you'll see a progress bar fill up.
*   Choose to stay on the dashboard to review all your sites, or open the new course in a new tab.
*   Observe your dashboard tray filling up with the new course site.

This area allows you to manage multiple courses efficiently.

Explore Course Management Options
---------------------------------

*   Click the three dots next to your course site for more options:
*   **Copy the space** to duplicate your course.
*   **Download the space** as a zip file for backup or offline use.
*   **Create a template** from your course after customizing settings, themes, and content. This lets you reuse the structure for future courses.
*   **Archive** the course if you want to remove it from the active dashboard but keep it stored.

Access and Edit Your New Course
-------------------------------

Click on the course site (e.g., **HAX100**) to enter the course editing environment. This is where you can begin adding content, selecting themes, and adjusting settings to tailor your course as needed.

Verification
------------

*   Check that your new course appears in the dashboard tray.
*   Ensure you can access the course editing environment and see the customization options.
*   Test downloading or templating features to confirm full access to management tools.

If all these steps work, your course is set up and ready for further customization!
