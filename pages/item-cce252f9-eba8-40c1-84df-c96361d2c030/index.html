Goal: Use HAX to author a course site or a structured resume/CV — an outline of pages with instructional content, assessments, and supporting media, or a cleanly outlined record of experience.

A dedicated concept walkthrough video for courses and resumes is coming soon. The tutorials and blocks below are enough to build either today.

Blocks & tutorials you'll use

  • Creating & Structuring Content — build the course or resume outline.
  • Import Content — convert an existing DOCX or spreadsheet into a structured site.
  • Instructional blocks — question types, self checks, stop notes, timelines, and tables for course content.
  • Column Layout and Learning Component — structure pages for readability.

Course sites built in HAX are OER-friendly: the content is portable and can be remixed by others.
