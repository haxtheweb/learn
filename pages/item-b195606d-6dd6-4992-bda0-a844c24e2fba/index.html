Goal: Stand up a simple, maintainable website for a club, team, or community group — with a homepage, an outline of activities/pages, a way to show photos and meeting info, and no ongoing hosting lock-in.

A dedicated concept walkthrough video for club websites is coming soon. The tutorials and blocks below are enough to build a complete club site today.

Blocks & tutorials you'll use

  • HAXcms Dashboard — create the site from the dashboard.
  • Creating & Structuring Content — add pages for About, Meetings, Photos, Contact.
  • Working with Media — add a photo gallery and meeting videos.
  • Image Gallery and Collection List — present members and events.

Because HAX sites are portable files, the club owns its website and can move hosts at any time.
