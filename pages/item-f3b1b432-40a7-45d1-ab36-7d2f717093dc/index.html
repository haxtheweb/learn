Goal: Build a portfolio that shows off your work — art, writing, music, or projects — in a site you control and can take with you, without depending on a platform that might disappear.

A dedicated concept walkthrough video for portfolios is coming soon. The tutorials and blocks below are enough to build a complete portfolio today.

Blocks & tutorials you'll use

  • Editing & Authoring — assemble your story without writing code.
  • Working with Media — feature images and video of your work.
  • Image Gallery, Media Playlist, and Video Player — present visual and audio work.
  • Courses & Resumes — pair your portfolio with a resume or course evidence.

HAX's portable format means your portfolio is never held hostage by a host going out of business.
